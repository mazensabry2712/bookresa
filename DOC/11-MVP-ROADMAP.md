# BookResa — MVP Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation
Laravel 13, PHP 8.4, MySQL, auth, Blade layout, locale, theme, tenancy foundation, RBAC foundation, module catalog and documentation.

## Phase 1 — Platform Core
Users, tenants, memberships, business types, modules, plans, TenantResolver and Platform Admin foundation.

## Phase 2 — Business Operations
Business profile, services, staff, service assignment, business hours, breaks, holidays, special hours and staff availability.

## Phase 3 — Booking Engine
Customers, public business page, service/staff selection, availability, booking lifecycle, calendar, booking reference, concurrency protection and tenant isolation tests.

## Phase 4 — Subscription and Usage
Plans, subscriptions, pricing controls, unique customer counting, usage charges, owner usage display, upgrade/downgrade/renewal/expiry and pricing snapshots.

## Phase 5 — Payments and Notifications
Payment Core, Kashier adapter, secure checkout/session, signed webhook, idempotency, booking/subscription payment state and email/database notifications.

## Phase 6 — Admin and Production
Platform Admin dashboard, businesses, plans, subscriptions, usage, revenue, audit logs, rate limits, backups, logging and performance hardening.

## Outside MVP unless separately approved
Branches, inventory, CRM, recurring bookings, advanced analytics, WhatsApp/SMS, mobile apps, complex tax/invoicing and marketplace payouts.
